# Privacy Policy – Ravel

**Effective date: August 1, 2026**

## 1. Data Controller

The data controller is Andrzej Chmiel, publisher of the Ravel mobile app.

Contact: andret2344@gmail.com

---

## 2. Data Collected

### 2.1 Account data

Ravel processes personal data when the user chooses to sign in with a Google account (OAuth 2.0), handled through Firebase Authentication.

When signing in, the app receives:

- the user's email address
- a unique account identifier (Firebase UID)
- the display name and profile picture associated with the Google account, if the user has one

No other information from the Google account is requested or accessed. Ravel does not read the user's contacts, calendar, files, photos, or any other Google service data, and never receives the user's Google password.

Signing in is optional. The app can be played without an account, in which case no account data is collected and game progress stays on the device.

### 2.2 Data collected automatically

When the app connects to Firebase services, the following is processed regardless of whether the user is signed in:

- IP address
- device model, operating system version, and language
- app version and a randomly generated installation identifier
- crash reports and diagnostic information
- basic usage events, such as app launches, screens opened, and levels completed

### 2.3 Advertising identifiers

If the user consents to personalised advertising, the app also processes the Android Advertising ID and approximate, IP-derived location for the purpose of ad delivery and measurement. Without that consent, only non-personalised advertising is shown and no advertising identifier is used for profiling.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Processing

Data is processed using Firebase (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC), which acts as a data processor under the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum.

Firebase infrastructure may store and process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers take place on the basis of the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, as set out in Google's terms.

Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## 4. Game Data and Leaderboards

For signed-in users, the app stores gameplay data linked to the account, such as completed levels, daily puzzle results, times, and statistics. This keeps progress synchronised across the user's devices and restores it after reinstalling the app.

Ravel also offers leaderboards. A user who takes part in a leaderboard has their results shown publicly to other players of the app, next to a nickname they choose themselves in the app.

The nickname is the only thing other players see. It is not taken from the Google account: neither the name nor the profile picture attached to that account is ever shown to other users, and neither is the email address.

Users who do not wish to appear publicly can play without signing in, or turn the leaderboard off in the app's settings.

---

## 5. Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used: which levels are played, where players stop, and which crashes occur. Analytics data is used in aggregate to improve the app. It is not used to identify individual users and is not sold.

---

## 6. Advertising

The app shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob, and only as rewarded advertising: an ad is played when the user chooses to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. Ravel does not show banners, interstitials, or any advertising the user did not ask for.

Before any personalised advertising is shown, users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent through a consent management platform. Consent can be withdrawn or changed at any time in the app's settings.

Ad providers may process device identifiers and interaction data to deliver and measure ads. Google's information on how it uses data from apps that use its services is available at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 8. Data Sharing

Personal data is not sold.

Data is shared only with the following processors and providers, and only to the extent needed to run the app:

- Google (Firebase Authentication, Firebase database and hosting services, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics)
- Google AdMob and its advertising partners, for the delivery and measurement of advertising

Data may also be disclosed where required by law.

---

## 9. Retention Period and Deletion

Data is stored for as long as the user keeps an account in the app.

To delete an account and all data associated with it, send a request by email to andret2344@gmail.com from the address the account was created with. The app links to this policy from the account settings screen. Requests are handled within 30 days, after which the account identifier, the linked game data, and the user's leaderboard entries are permanently removed.

Analytics and crash data are retained in aggregated or pseudonymised form according to Firebase's default retention settings, currently up to 14 months.

Users who play without signing in store no account data on the server. Local game data is removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

---

## 10. User Rights

Users have the right to:

- access their data
- request deletion, correction, or restriction of processing
- object to processing based on legitimate interest
- withdraw consent given for analytics or personalised advertising
- request a copy of their data in a portable format
- file a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl)

Requests are made by email at andret2344@gmail.com.

---

## 11. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored under Firebase's security model, with database access restricted by security rules so that a signed-in user can reach only their own data. Authentication tokens are held only for as long as the session requires.

---

## 12. Children

Ravel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If such data is identified, it is deleted.

---
