# Privacy Policy – Ravel

**Effective date: November 15, 2026**

## 1. Data Controller

The data controller is Andrzej Chmiel, publisher of the Ravel mobile app.

Contact: andret2344@gmail.com

---

## 2. Summary

Ravel has no user accounts and no server of its own. The game, its dictionaries and your progress live on your device. Three things send data off the device: the advertising, which is shown only when you choose to watch an ad, together with its consent form; usage statistics about how the game is played; and crash reports. The last two can each be turned off in the app's options.

---

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

The app keeps the following on your device:

- game progress: levels completed, daily puzzle results and streak (including missed days you chose to save, or chose not to), the words of each solved daily puzzle, the board in progress, times, and hints used
- boards built in advance, so the next puzzle opens without waiting
- the coin balance, how many ads were watched today, whether today's free gift was claimed, and whether today's hint suggestion was shown
- settings: language, theme, vibration, level modifiers such as hiding the word lengths, whether to be reminded of an unsolved daily puzzle, whether the tutorial was completed, whether the list of a solved board's words is shown open, whether usage statistics and crash reports are sent, and your advertising consent choice
- the daily puzzle notification: whether it is on, whether its time is chosen automatically or the time you picked, the time of day you last solved the daily puzzle (which the automatic time follows), the last day a notification was sent and how many in a row went unanswered, and whether you have been asked about turning it on. The notification is scheduled and shown by the app on your device; nothing about it is sent anywhere.
- for usage statistics and crash reports (section 5): the identifiers Google Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics generate for this installation, and statistics or reports not yet sent

Apart from what section 5 describes being sent, this data is not sent to the publisher or to any server operated by the publisher. It is removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, this data can be included in your device's backup, which is stored by Google under your Google account and governed by Google's privacy policy. You control this in your device settings, not in the app.

---

## 4. Advertising

The app shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob, and only as rewarded advertising: an ad is played when you choose to watch one in exchange for in-game coins or for saving your daily streak. Ravel does not show banners, interstitials, or any advertising you did not ask for.

When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes, on Google's side:

- the IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
- device information, such as model, operating system version and language
- the Android Advertising ID, if you consented to personalized advertising (the app declares the advertising ID permission for this purpose)
- interactions with the ad, and diagnostic information about the SDK

Before any personalized advertising is shown, users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent management platform (User Messaging Platform). The form offers to consent to all purposes at once, or to choose purposes and partners one by one under "Manage options", where consent can be refused. Without that consent, only non-personalized advertising is shown. Consent can be changed at any time in the app's options.

Google's information on how it uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## 5. Usage Statistics and Crash Reports

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, both provided by Google, to find out how the game is played and what goes wrong in it, so that it can be improved.

Usage statistics record what happens in the game: how long a board took to build on your device and whether it was built in time, levels and daily puzzles finished with their times and the hints used, whether the tutorial was finished or skipped, hints you could not afford, the "stuck?" suggestion shown or followed, rewarded ads requested, delivered and watched, saved streaks, opening the game from its notification, and level modifiers turned on or off. Along with them Google Analytics collects an identifier generated for this installation of the app, the device model, operating system and app version, language, and an approximate location (country and region) derived from the IP address. It does not collect the advertising ID: the app turns that off. Nothing you see or do on a board is recorded beyond what is listed here; the words, the grid and your moves on it are not sent.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, usage statistics follow the choice you make in the consent form described in section 4 (Google's consent mode). Without your consent, Google Analytics stores no identifier on your device and sends only measurements without one, which Google uses in aggregate to estimate the statistics of users who did not consent.

Crash reports are sent when the app crashes: a technical description of the error (the stack trace), the device model, operating system and app version, free memory and storage at the time of the crash, the time, and an identifier generated for this installation of the app by Crashlytics. They do not depend on the consent form.

Both can be turned off at any time in the app's options, under "Privacy settings": "Usage statistics" and "Crash reports". Turned off, nothing of that kind is sent at all.

Google's information on how it uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 6. Purpose and Legal Basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Showing a rewarded ad you asked for, non-personalized | Legitimate interest of the controller, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR |
| Personalized advertising | Consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR |
| Usage statistics, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, with your consent | Consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR |
| Usage statistics without an identifier, where consent was not given | Legitimate interest of the controller in improving the game, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR |
| Crash reports | Legitimate interest of the controller in finding and fixing errors, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR |

Consent given for personalized advertising or for usage statistics can be withdrawn at any time in the app's options, with effect for the future. You can object to processing based on legitimate interest by turning off usage statistics or crash reports in the same place.

## 7. Data Sharing and Transfers

Personal data is not sold.

Tapping a word in the list of a solved board's words opens its meaning on sjp.pl (Polish) or Wiktionary (English) in your browser, and the link under the reminder settings opens dontkillmyapp.com at the page for your phone's manufacturer. That site receives the request from your browser, as for any page you open, under its own privacy policy; the app itself sends nothing.

The only data shared is the advertising data described in section 4 and the usage statistics and crash reports described in section 5, which are processed by Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC) to deliver and measure ads and, as a processor for the publisher, to provide the statistics and crash reports. Google may process it outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, on the basis of the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, as set out in Google's terms.

Data may also be disclosed where required by law.

---

## 8. Retention

The publisher runs no server of its own. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Usage statistics are kept in Google Analytics for 14 months; what is linked to the identifier of an installation still in use is kept until 14 months after that installation was last used. Crash reports are kept in Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days. Advertising data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

---

## 9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- access your data
- request deletion, correction, or restriction of processing
- object to processing based on legitimate interest
- withdraw consent given for personalized advertising or usage statistics
- request a copy of your data in a portable format
- file a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl)

Requests can be made by email at andret2344@gmail.com. For data processed by Google for advertising, Google's own tools apply as well, including resetting or deleting the advertising ID in your device settings.

---

## 10. Security

Advertising traffic, usage statistics and crash reports are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Game data stored on the device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

## 11. Children

Ravel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
