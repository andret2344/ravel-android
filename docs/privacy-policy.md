# Privacy Policy – Ravel

**Effective date: November 11, 2026**

## 1. Data Controller

The data controller is Andrzej Chmiel, publisher of the Ravel mobile app.

Contact: andret2344@gmail.com

---

## 2. Summary

Ravel has no user accounts, no server of its own, and no analytics. The game, its dictionaries and your progress live on your device. The only part of the app that sends data off the device is the advertising, which is shown only when you choose to watch an ad, and the consent form that comes with it.

---

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

The app keeps the following on your device only:

- game progress: levels completed, daily puzzle results and streak (including missed days you chose to save, or chose not to), the board in progress, times, and hints used
- boards built in advance, so the next puzzle opens without waiting
- the coin balance, how many ads were watched today, whether today's free gift was claimed, and whether today's hint suggestion was shown
- settings: language, theme, vibration, hard mode, whether to be reminded of an unsolved daily puzzle, whether the tutorial was completed, and your advertising consent choice

This data is not sent to the publisher or to any server operated by the publisher. It is removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, this data can be included in your device's backup, which is stored by Google under your Google account and governed by Google's privacy policy. You control this in your device settings, not in the app.

---

## 4. Advertising

The app shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob, and only as rewarded advertising: an ad is played when you choose to watch one in exchange for in-game coins or for saving your daily streak. Ravel does not show banners, interstitials, or any advertising you did not ask for.

When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes, on Google's side:

- the IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
- device information, such as model, operating system version and language
- the Android Advertising ID, if you consented to personalised advertising (the app declares the advertising ID permission for this purpose)
- interactions with the ad, and diagnostic information about the SDK

Before any personalised advertising is shown, users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent management platform (User Messaging Platform). Without that consent, only non-personalised advertising is shown. Consent can be changed at any time in the app's options.

Google's information on how it uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## 5. Purpose and Legal Basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Showing a rewarded ad you asked for, non-personalised | Legitimate interest of the controller, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR |
| Personalised advertising | Consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR |

Consent given for personalised advertising can be withdrawn at any time in the app's options, with effect for the future.

---

## 6. Data Sharing and Transfers

Personal data is not sold.

The only data shared is the advertising data described in section 4, which is processed by Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC) to deliver and measure ads. Google may process it outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, on the basis of the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, as set out in Google's terms.

Data may also be disclosed where required by law.

---

## 7. Retention

The publisher holds no personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for the publisher to retain or delete. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Advertising data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

---

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- access your data
- request deletion, correction, or restriction of processing
- object to processing based on legitimate interest
- withdraw consent given for personalised advertising
- request a copy of your data in a portable format
- file a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl)

Requests can be made by email at andret2344@gmail.com. For data processed by Google for advertising, Google's own tools apply as well, including resetting or deleting the advertising ID in your device settings.

---

## 9. Security

Advertising traffic is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Game data stored on the device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

---

## 10. Children

Ravel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

---

## 11. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated, in particular before any feature that processes more data, such as accounts, cloud sync, leaderboards, or analytics, is released. The current version is always available at the address where this document is published, and the effective date above is updated on every change.
